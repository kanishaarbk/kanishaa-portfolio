STRESSGUARD / SENSEIT

StressGuard — Federated Stress Detection

An ongoing stress-detection project planned around the SWELL-KW dataset. The goal is to investigate physiological and available interaction features while training across participant-based clients without centralising raw participant data.

Work and planned modules

  • Audit labels, missing values, participant IDs, timestamps, and potential leakage before training.
  • Build an ANN/MLP stress classifier and evaluate it with suitable classification metrics.
  • Explore client clustering, ClusterGAN representations, cluster-wise FedAvg, FedProx, and MMD-based drift detection.
  • Connect the ML workflow to a React/Capacitor app and Django REST backend as the architecture develops.
PythonPyTorchFederated LearningFedAvgFedProxReactDjango REST

Ongoing project. Planned components are not presented as fully verified or production-ready.
